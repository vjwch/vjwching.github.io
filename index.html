HOW YOU GOT HERE

You are locked in Room 404. The door does not open, and the hotel does not mean to let you leave.

Your roommate decides. Mr. Smiles is the night concierge — and he shares this room with you. Every checkout needs his signature, and he only signs for a guest he is pleased with.

So keep him happy. His rating is the lock on your door. Raise it and he signs you out at dawn. Drop it and you stay.

You checked in eleven months ago — the week your brother Peter vanished here. You remember none of it. Peter is still here. So are you.
